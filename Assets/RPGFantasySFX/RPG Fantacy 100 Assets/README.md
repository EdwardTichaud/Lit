# 100 RPG & Fantasy Sound Effects Pack

## Overview
A collection of RPG and fantasy sound effects organized into themed folders for use in Unity projects.

## Folder categories
The pack is organized into categories including weapons and combat, creature sounds, magic effects, doors and mechanisms, inventory and equipment, books and artifacts, shields and armor, and related fantasy gameplay effects.

## Import and use
1. Import this package into your Unity project.
2. Find the audio clips in the `RPG Fantacy 100 Assets` folder and its category subfolders.
3. Select a clip to preview it in the Inspector.
4. Drag an AudioClip into an Audio Source component or assign it in your own scripts.
5. For spatial sounds, configure the Audio Source's spatial blend, rolloff, and distance to suit your game.

## Audio details
The included WAV files retain their original sample rate and channel count. Audio clips that peaked too close to digital full scale have been attenuated to a maximum peak of -1 dBFS to provide headroom and reduce clipping risk. Audio importer settings are configured to use PCM compression for the clips.

## Notes
This package contains audio assets; it does not require custom scripts to play the clips. Preview each sound in your target project and adjust playback settings to fit your game.
